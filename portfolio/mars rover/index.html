This page is about the 1/4 scale Mars Rover I am making.

At Lake Tuggeranong College, we are making a bunch of 1/4 scale Mars Rovers.

We give names to the Rovers, and we base them off of character from the show Bluey, I am working on Chilli.

I am currently working on the electronics side of Chilli currently, the frame and rocker system is fully built.
